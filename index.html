05Good to know

A few honest answers.

Is Blink & Break free?

Yes. The current extension is free, with no account and no ads.

Does it collect my browsing data?

No browsing-history or webpage-access permission appears in the extension manifest. Blink & Break uses alarms, notifications, and storage.

Can I customize the intervals?

Yes. The reminder interval can be 1–1440 minutes, breaks can be 5–300 seconds, and snooze can be 1–120 minutes.

What happens when I pause?

The remaining time is stored. When you resume, the timer continues from that point instead of starting over.

What is Eye Buddy?

Eye Buddy is the small in-extension companion. Completed breaks earn XP, and enough XP raises its level.

Does it work offline?

The timer, notifications, settings, and progress logic live inside the extension and do not require a Blink & Break account or remote service.
